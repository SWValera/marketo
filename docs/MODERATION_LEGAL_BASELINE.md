This is technical source metadata, not a legal opinion or certification. No
LAW/REGULATION auto-blocks are enabled in the initial ruleset. JEVU policy is
intentionally distinguished from criminal/regulatory classification.

Official sources consulted via Әділет (including its official old.adilet.zan.kz

requests timed out. Indexed official excerpts establish relevant topics but do
not certify every consolidated amendment/effective-date combination. The full
current consolidated versions and applicability remain LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED
before public compliance launch. Do not activate new legal prohibitions from

finding says “illegal by law” when its authority is JEVU_POLICY. A legal reviewer
must record applicable provisions, temporal scope, exemptions, owner approval
and effective dates before promoting a draft legal rule to active.
